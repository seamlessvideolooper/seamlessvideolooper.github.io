Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

What the app does

SeamlessVideoLooper creates seamless looping videos entirely on your device. All video processing is performed locally — your videos never leave your phone.

On-device processing

Video analysis, loop-point detection, blending, and rendering all happen on your device. No video data is uploaded to any server.

Optional data collection

The app collects data only when you explicitly choose to:

  • Feedback reports (user-initiated): When you tap "Send Feedback," the app may include device model, Android version, app version, device tier, a log snippet, current step, settings, video metadata, quality metrics, and an optional screenshot. You can uncheck any item before sending.
  • Crash reports (opt-out): Automatic crash reports are enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. Crash reports include the stack trace, thread name, and device information.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Firebase services solely for feedback and crash report submission:

  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in)
  • Firebase Firestore (report storage)
  • Firebase Storage (optional screenshot upload)

No other third-party services, analytics SDKs, or advertising SDKs are used.

Data retention

Feedback reports are stored in Firebase Firestore. Local screenshot cache on your device expires after 30 days. You can disable crash sharing at any time in Settings.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

SeamlessVideoLooper does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels. The app does not track your usage patterns or browsing behavior.

Data deletion
